# Question

Describe your approach to building scalable and resilient cloud-native applications.

## Good Response

A good response should demonstrate:
- Understanding of cloud-native principles (microservices, containers, orchestration)
- Specific technologies and platforms used (e.g., Kubernetes, Docker, AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Strategies for horizontal and vertical scaling
- Resilience patterns (circuit breakers, retries, fallbacks)
- Monitoring and observability practices
- Real-world examples from previous projects

Example: "Our approach leverages containerized microservices deployed on Kubernetes clusters. We implement auto-scaling based on CPU and memory metrics, use circuit breakers for inter-service communication, and maintain comprehensive observability through distributed tracing and centralized logging. In our last project, this architecture handled a 10x traffic spike during a product launch with zero downtime."

## What to Look For

- **Depth of technical knowledge**: Do they understand modern cloud-native patterns?
- **Practical experience**: Are they providing specific examples or generic descriptions?
- **Scalability strategy**: Clear explanation of how the system scales under load
- **Resilience patterns**: Understanding of failure modes and mitigation strategies
- **Monitoring approach**: Evidence of proactive system health management
- **Technology choices**: Appropriate tool selection with justification
