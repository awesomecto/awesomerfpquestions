# Question

Please provide your pricing model and explain how costs scale with usage or additional services.

## Good Response

A comprehensive pricing response should include:
- Clear breakdown of pricing components (license fees, implementation, support, etc.)
- Volume discounts or tiered pricing structures
- Additional costs (training, customization, integrations)
- Payment terms and billing frequency
- Price lock guarantees or escalation clauses
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) analysis
- Comparison to alternative solutions (if applicable)
- Sample scenarios showing costs at different scales

Example: "Our pricing consists of three components: (1) Platform license at $500/user/month with volume discounts starting at 50 users (15% off) and 100+ users (25% off), (2) One-time implementation fee of $50,000 including data migration and 40 hours of training, (3) Annual support at 18% of license fees. Additional integrations are priced at $5,000-$15,000 per integration. We offer monthly or annual billing with 10% discount for annual prepayment. Prices are locked for 3 years with maximum 5% annual escalation thereafter. For a 75-user deployment, Year 1 TCO would be approximately $500K."

## What to Look For

- **Transparency**: All costs clearly itemized, no hidden fees
- **Flexibility**: Multiple pricing tiers or customizable options
- **Scalability**: Clear understanding of how costs change with growth
- **Competitiveness**: Pricing aligned with market rates and value delivered
- **Terms clarity**: Payment terms, contract length, cancellation policies
- **Value justification**: Clear explanation of ROI or cost savings
- **TCO analysis**: Understanding of full ownership costs beyond license fees
- **Comparison basis**: Benchmarking against alternatives or industry standards
