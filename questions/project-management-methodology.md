# Question

Describe your project management methodology and how you ensure projects are delivered on time and within budget.

## Good Response

A strong response should include:
- Specific methodology used (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, hybrid)
- Project planning and estimation techniques
- Risk management processes
- Change management procedures
- Communication and reporting cadence
- Tools and platforms used for project tracking
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Examples of successful project delivery

Example: "We use an Agile/Scrum methodology with 2-week sprints. Our process includes detailed sprint planning with story point estimation, daily standups, and retrospectives. We use Jira for backlog management and tracking, and Confluence for documentation. Risk registers are reviewed weekly, and we maintain a 15% buffer for unexpected changes. We provide weekly status reports to stakeholders and hold monthly steering committee meetings. Our on-time delivery rate over the past 2 years is 94%, with 89% of projects coming in at or under budget."

## What to Look For

- **Methodology clarity**: Clear explanation of the approach, not just buzzwords
- **Adaptability**: Evidence they can adjust methodology to project needs
- **Risk management**: Proactive identification and mitigation strategies
- **Communication**: Structured approach to stakeholder engagement
- **Tools and processes**: Established systems for tracking and visibility
- **Metrics and accountability**: How they measure success and handle issues
- **Real examples**: Specific examples demonstrating their capabilities
- **Team involvement**: How team members participate in planning and estimation
- **Change control**: Process for handling scope changes and their impact
